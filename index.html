ABOUT THE ATELIER

Designing Spaces Around the Way You Live.

"True luxury is not about excess. It is about harmony, light, natural textures, and the quiet comfort of spaces calibrated to your daily rhythm."

At Atelier Luxe, we combine the refined restraint of international interior architecture with the rich warmth of modern Indian living. Every home is approached as a bespoke canvas where natural materials, custom millwork, and balanced illumination come together.

From bare-shell spatial reconfiguration to modular kitchen engineering and bespoke furniture curation, our studio ensures every square foot reflects your personal lifestyle with uncompromising craftsmanship.

      OUR METHODOLOGY

      A Structured Four-Step Design Journey

      From initial brief to final white-glove handover, our phased process guarantees transparency and precision.

      01

      Discover

      We conduct an in-depth spatial and lifestyle audit to understand your aesthetic aspirations, circulation habits, and functional necessities.

      02

      Design

      We engineer 2D flow plans, curate material sample boards, and produce photorealistic 3D perspectives illustrating textures and lighting.

      03

      Refine

      Through collaborative feedback, we fine-tune millwork details, lighting points, hardware choices, and generate an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ).

      04

      Deliver

      We manage factory production, site execution, quality audits, and deliver a fully cleaned, polished home ready for living.

      COMMON QUERIES

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Answers to questions about our scope, turnkey execution, and design process.

      In our Design-Only packages (such as Essential and Premium), our studio delivers comprehensive space planning, 2D layouts, material palettes, 3D renderings, and detailed working drawings so you or your contractors can execute. In Complete Home Interiors (Turnkey), our studio oversees full civil contracting, modular factory joinery, electrical, painting, and on-site delivery from start to finish.

      Our core studio operates across South Mumbai (Worli, Malabar Hill, Cuffe Parade), Bandra, Khar, Juhu, Lower Parel, Prabhadevi, BKC, and Alibaug luxury estates. We also undertake selective outstation commissions by appointment.

      Yes, absolutely. Every project is unique. Our packages provide a clear starting baseline, and you can easily add specific spaces (such as home cinema, private bar, terrace landscape, or additional 3D renders) via our itemized rate options.

      During the design phase, we produce a comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BOQ) that lists every item—from linear feet of modular cabinetry and quartz countertops to lighting fixtures and civil works—with clear unit prices and material specs. There are no hidden surprises.

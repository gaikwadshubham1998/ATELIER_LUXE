CASE STUDY OVERVIEW

Architectural Vision & Spatial Strategy

Designed for an art connoisseur family, The Serene Pavilion transforms a bare-shell duplex into an expansive, light-drenched sanctuary.

The Design Challenge

Maximizing natural cross-ventilation while managing harsh afternoon sun exposure, alongside integrating a museum-grade climate and art lighting system without visible conduits.

Our Design Solution

We engineered custom motorized architectural louvers clad in champagne-brushed anodized aluminum, coupled with recessed low-glare magnetic track fixtures that frame artwork subtly without heating delicate canvases.

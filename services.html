Our Interior Architecture Disciplines

Specialized design and turnkey execution services tailored to modern Indian residences, penthouses, and executive workplaces.

RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE

Full Home Interior Design & Space Planning

Comprehensive interior architecture for bare-shell or pre-owned apartments and penthouses. We optimize structural layout, acoustic zoning, natural light flow, and surface materiality across the entire residence.

As-built 2D CAD floor plans
Photorealistic 3D perspectives
Wall finishes & paint curation
Detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ)
CULINARY SPACES

Ergonomic Modular Kitchen Design

Designed for avid cooks who love to host. We integrate high-performance sintered porcelain countertops (Dekton/Silestone), Blum motorized motion hardware, and segregated wet heavy-cooking and dry entertaining zones.

Work triangle ergonomic engineering
Anti-fingerprint nanotech shutters
Pantry & internal drawer pull-outs
Chimney & hob ducting specifications
SANCTUARIES

Master Bedroom & Walk-in Dressing Suites

The primary suite reimagined as a five-star private cocoon. Custom fluted timber acoustic panels drop noise levels, while glass-encased walk-in dressing wardrobes feature internal sensor lighting and leather-lined jewelry organizers.

Bespoke bedhead & channel upholstery
Bronze-framed fluted glass wardrobes
Acoustic perimeter insulation design
En-suite master vanity coordination
ILLUMINATION

False Ceiling & Architectural Lighting Planning

Ditch glaring center downlights. We formulate low-glare magnetic track channels, seamless cove glow, accent art spotlights, and smart circadian dimming that shifts from crisp day reading light to warm amber evening ambience.

Reflected Ceiling Plans (RCP)
Magnetic track & COB spot layout
AC plenum & trap door concealment
Circuit load balancing & switch points
TURNKEY DELIVERY

Turnkey Contracting & Site Management

The single-window solution. We take absolute responsibility for civil demolition, masonry, electrical rewiring, plumbing, tile/marble laying, factory millwork assembly, and final handover. No dealing with chaotic contractor disputes.

Dedicated on-site project engineer
Weekly WhatsApp photo & video logs
Strict milestone delivery timelines
Deep chemical cleaning before handover

Studio Engagement & Consultation Guidelines

1. Scope of Preliminary Estimates

The project calculator and rate figures presented on this website are indicative benchmarks provided for preliminary budgeting purposes. They do not constitute a legally binding quotation. A formal contract with a verified Bill of Quantities (BOQ) is issued following an on-site physical survey and formal scope definition.

2. Intellectual Property & Design Copyright

All floor plans, 3D renderings, mood boards, elevations, technical drawings, and architectural concepts produced by Atelier Luxe remain the intellectual property of the studio until the full design fee has been settled according to the milestone schedule.

3. Turnkey Execution & Warranties

For turnkey contracts, materials and factory modular joinery are covered by a 10-year manufacturer structural warranty against delamination and termite degradation. Hardware warranties (such as Blum, Hettich, Hafele) are backed directly by the respective manufacturers.

4. Governing Law

All studio engagements and contracts shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Mumbai, Maharashtra.

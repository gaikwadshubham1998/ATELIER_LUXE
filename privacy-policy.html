Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

Atelier Luxe Interior Architecture respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy statement explains how we handle inquiries, personal information, and project documentation when you interact with our website or services.

2. Information We Collect

When you complete our Project Inquiry Form, we collect details that you voluntarily supply, including your full name, telephone / WhatsApp number, optional email address, property type, location, approximate square footage, and design preferences.

3. How Information Is Processed

Our inquiry forms use direct client-side WhatsApp routing. When you click "Send Inquiry on WhatsApp", the information entered is compiled into a text message and passed to WhatsApp's official click-to-chat API. We do not sell, rent, or lease your contact information to third-party advertisers.

4. Photography & Client Confidentiality

Photographs of completed projects published on our website are displayed only with the prior written authorization of our clients. Exact apartment numbers, building identities, and private client identities are kept strictly confidential unless expressly consented.

5. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries or to request data removal, please contact our concierge at concierge@atelierluxe.in.
